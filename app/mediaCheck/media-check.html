Linux package checks

Snap must expose both interfaces:

snap connections windows-app-for-linux sudo snap connect windows-app-for-linux:camera sudo snap connect windows-app-for-linux:audio-record

Flatpak must expose audio and video devices:

flatpak info --show-permissions io.github.mariuszkopowski.WindowsAppForLinux

Close other apps that may hold the camera, and verify the selected input in your desktop sound settings.

Passing this test proves the wrapper can capture locally. In the remote-session toolbar, enable Microphone and Camera, then reconnect. The host must also allow audiocapturemode:i:1 and camerastoredirect:s:*.
